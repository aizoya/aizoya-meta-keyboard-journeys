THE PROBLEM

A working checkout can still exclude a keyboard customer.

Mouse testing can miss focus-order and keyboard-activation failures. Recorded scripts can also become stale when controls or availability change.

Teams need evidence they can inspect and replay, without turning one automated attempt into an accessibility certification.

THE VERIFIED COMPARISON

Same adaptive fixture. Two different outcomes.

0 model calls

Saved route: incomplete

A previously successful five-key route replayed unchanged. Sand became unavailable. The route reached Blue, moved past it without activation and ended incomplete.

6 bounded requests

Muse Spark: completed

Muse consumed fresh structured state, selected the remaining valid option and completed. There was no retry.

Claim boundary: this shows one saved sequence becoming stale. It does not prove that every deterministic policy fails or that the model generalizes beyond the owned fixture.
