## Recording checklist

- Begin on the headline; show Meta evidence within 12 seconds.
- Keep the browser at readable zoom and hide unrelated tabs.
- Use the scripted runner during recording; do not spend on a new model call.
- Show the committed adaptive result before the workflow page so signed-out judges retain access after artifact expiry.
- Record one short physical-iPhone clip or screenshot to establish the judge flow works on touch.
- Confirm the public repository and video open without sign-in.
- Export at 1080p when practical; verify audio, captions, URLs and total duration.
